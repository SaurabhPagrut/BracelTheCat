# YouTube: the intro and demo video

Made by `npm run video` (with `npm run serve` running): `dist/video/bracel-demo.webm` (1920×1080, 30 fps, 78 s, no
sound) and `dist/video/thumbnail.jpg` (1280×720), copied to `store/bracel-demo.webm` (kept out of git: about 10 MB,
rebuilt by the script) and `store/youtube-thumbnail.jpg`. YouTube takes the WebM as it is.

Scenes: title card, news page (walks, jumps), dashboard (grooms, kneads, yarn, naps), web app (hangs, drops), docs
(stroked, pounces), a dialog opening (it leaves), dark page (the five coats, growing), the popup (Show brings it),
privacy card, end card. The Bento Cat credit is on every card.

The video has no sound track. In YouTube Studio → the video → **Editor** → **Audio**, add a calm track from the
YouTube Audio Library (free to use on YouTube). Keep it quiet; the captions carry the story.

## Title (100 characters max)

Bracel – A Cute Virtual Cat for Every Website (Browser Extension Demo)

## Description

Meet Bracel, a cozy virtual cat and browser pet that lives on the websites you visit.

It sits on the tops of boxes and pictures, walks along them, jumps from one to the next, hangs under headers, grooms,
kneads, plays with a ball of yarn and naps while you read, study or work. Stroke it with your pointer and it purrs.
Wiggle the pointer above it and it pounces.

It never gets in the way: it takes no clicks, no focus and no keys, and it steps aside when a dialog opens.

🐾 Add Bracel to Chrome (free): https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/bracel-a-cat-for-every-we/diiopbiepmlejbbibggfhhdmnaeiehjf
🌐 Website: https://bracel.saurabhpagrut.in
🔒 Privacy: https://bracel.saurabhpagrut.in/privacy (collects nothing, sends nothing)
💻 Source: https://github.com/SaurabhPagrut/BracelTheCat

Five coats (Cloud, Slate, Slate tuxedo, Accent blue, Amber), any size, a purr you can turn on or off, and 22 actions
to pick from. Bring it to any tab with one click or Alt+Shift+B.
