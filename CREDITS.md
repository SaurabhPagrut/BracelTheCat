# Credits

## Art

Bracel's look is adapted from Bento Cat by mikewirsch (rive.app/marketplace/21131-39716-bento-cat), licensed CC BY 4.0
(creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0). Redrawn and animated in code; walking, hanging, jumping, hiding, spots,
behaviour and coats are our own.

- Original: "Bento Cat" by mikewirsch, https://rive.app/marketplace/21131-39716-bento-cat/
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- What we adapted: the head, ears, eyes with two catchlights, the ω mouth, the colours and the base poses.
- What we changed: no `.riv` file or Rive runtime is used. Bracel is drawn in code on a canvas, with new coats and new
  walking, hanging, jumping, hiding, spots and behaviour.
- mikewirsch does not endorse this extension.
