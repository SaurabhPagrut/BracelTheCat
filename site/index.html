A cat for every website

Bracel lives on the edges of the pages you visit. It walks, naps, grooms and purrs, and never gets in the way. It's on this page right now.

Add to Chrome Free · works in Chrome and Edge · no account

Say hello

Stroke Bracel with your pointer and it purrs. Click it three times and it gets annoyed. Or pick a coat and an action:

Never in the way

It takes no clicks, no focus and no keys. It leaves when a dialog opens and keeps clear of what you type in.

You choose where

Click the toolbar button to bring it to a tab, or switch it on for one site or every site. Your browser asks first.

Yours to dress

Five coats, any size, purring on or off, and 22 actions you can switch on, off, or play right now.

Private by design

Bracel collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. No analytics, no network requests, no account. Privacy policy
